GREEK LIFE & COMMUNITY

FRATERNITY & SORORITY LIFE

UMD's recognized organizations include 50+ social fraternities and sororities, all of which are culturally focused and share a commitment to brotherhood and sisterhood, service, scholarship, and philanthropy.

  • 4 Governing Councils: Interfraternity, Panhellenic, Multicultural, and National Pan-Hellenic Councils.
  • Philanthropy & Service: Raising hundreds of thousands of dollars for local and national charities annually.
  • Leadership Development: Access to executive board positions, alumni networking, and campus advocacy.

With over 800 student-run organizations on campus, there is a community for every interest, culture, academic field, and hobby at the University of Maryland.
