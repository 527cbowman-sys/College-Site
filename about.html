UNIVERSITY OVERVIEW

The State's Flagship Research University

Founded in 1856, the University of Maryland, College Park is a premier public land-grant research university located in College Park, Maryland—just 9 miles from downtown Washington, D.C. As the state’s flagship institution, UMD is home to over 40,000 scholars, world-renowned faculty, and pioneering research centers.

WHAT MAKES US UNIQUE

The Legend of Testudo & Terp Traditions

What makes UMD uniquely special is its rich mascot heritage and campus traditions. Our bronze Testudo statue outside McKeldin Library isn't just a symbol—it’s a living tradition. During final exams, thousands of students leave offerings ranging from coffee to lucky charms at Testudo's feet and rub his bronze nose for good luck!

As Maryland's only NCAA Division I Big Ten Conference university, Terp pride runs through everything we do—from our four-flag university emblem to the unmatched energy inside XFINITY Center.
