RESIDENCE HALLS & COMMUNITIES

HOUSING

Your home away from home. From bustling traditional halls on North Campus to suite and apartment-style living in South Campus, UMD offers supportive, vibrant residential communities designed for every Terp.

  • Living-Learning Programs: Connect deeply with peers in specialized academic cohorts.
  • 24/7 Security & Support: Dedicated resident assistants and campus support teams.
  • Prime Access: Live steps away from classrooms, dining halls, and recreation centers.

DINING

Fueling your ambition with diverse, sustainable, and flexible dining options across campus. Enjoy unlimited access dining halls, brand-name eateries, food trucks, and cozy cafes.

  • Anytime Dining Plans: Unlimited visits to all three major dining halls.
  • Dietary Accommodations: Robust allergen-aware, halal, kosher, and vegan offerings.
