WHAT WE LOOK FOR

Admissions Expectations

01

Academic Rigor

We evaluate your high school performance, GPA trends, and the challenge level of your coursework (AP, IB, Honors) to ensure academic readiness.

02

Holistic Review

Your application is evaluated as a whole. Letters of recommendation, essay depth, extracurricular involvement, and leadership hold substantial weight.

03

Test-Optional Policy

UMD offers a test-optional path. Applicants can choose whether to submit SAT/ACT scores without facing any disadvantage in holistic review.

AFFORDABILITY

Scholarships & Financial Aid

Over 75% of incoming freshmen receive financial support through merit-based scholarships, need-based grants, work-study programs, or financial assistance.

Banneker/Key Scholarship

UMD's most prestigious merit award, covering full tuition, room, board, and a book allowance.

Presidential Scholarships

Merit awards granted to high-achieving freshmen automatically evaluated upon application submission.

Need-Based Grants (FAFSA)

Federal and state aid awarded based on financial need submitted prior to the priority deadline.

Important Deadlines

Ensure your application and financial aid documents are submitted on time to maximize eligibility.

  • November 1 Early Action Application Deadline (Best consideration for merit awards)
  • January 1 FAFSA Priority Financial Aid Submission
  • Regular Decision January 20 Application Deadline
